what is router-outlet
---------------------
router-outlet is a directive provided by Angular Router that acts as a placeholder for routed components. 
Based on the current URL and route configuration, Angular dynamically renders the corresponding component inside the router outlet. 
It allows us to navigate between components without reloading the entire page, which is an important part of Angular's SPA architecture. 
For example, in my application I keep the Header and Footer outside the router outlet, while Login, Dashboard, Profile, and other pages are dynamically rendered inside it.
